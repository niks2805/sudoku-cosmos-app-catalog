# Privacy Policy for Sudoku Cosmos

**Effective date:** 17 September 2026  
**Developer:** Nikhil K Thakur  
**Privacy contact:** niks28.consult@gmail.com

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Nikhil K Thakur ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use **Sudoku Cosmos** (the "App"). Sudoku Cosmos is a puzzle game designed for a general audience aged 13 and above. It is **not directed to children under 13**.

The App is designed to remain playable without an account and to retain core game progress on your device. We collect only the information needed to provide optional support functions, operate advertising after it is enabled in a released version, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

## 2. Information the App handles

### A. Game data stored on your device

Sudoku Cosmos stores game information locally on your device. This can include saved puzzle state, completed levels, world selection, game settings, reward-wallet balances, streaks, Cipher case progress, and feature-presentation preferences. This information is used to preserve your play experience between sessions.

Core local game data is not sent to us merely because you play the game. You can remove it by using any available in-app reset control or by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App through your device settings. Clearing or uninstalling the App may permanently remove locally stored progress that is not backed up by your device or operating system.

### B. Feedback and support requests

The App may offer optional Feedback and Support forms. If you choose to submit one, we may receive the message you write, its selected category or support path, your optional email address if you provide one, the App version, your device platform, and the screen context needed to understand the issue.

We use this information to respond when a reply is requested, investigate bugs, improve the App, and maintain a support history. Feedback and support data is transmitted only when you expressly submit the form. It may be processed through a Google-hosted support record or another service provider used to operate our support workflow.

### C. Advertising and consent information

A future released version of Sudoku Cosmos may use the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google AdMob to show optional rewarded advertisements and a limited interstitial advertisement. Before advertisements are requested where consent is required, the App will use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request, store, and honor applicable privacy choices.

When advertising is enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the following categories of data for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
- device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app-set ID where available;
- product interaction information, such as App launches, taps, ad views, and video views; and
- diagnostic and performance information, such as App launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.

Ad requests will be made only after the App's consent flow permits them. Where required, users can revisit privacy choices from an accessible in-App privacy-options control. You may also reset or delete your Android advertising ID through your device settings. Google describes its handling of data through its own privacy documentation.

The App's planned advertising design does not place ads inside active Sudoku boards, Cipher investigations, evidence reveals, suspect predictions, or onboarding. Rewarded ads are optional and are offered only after a player makes a clear choice to receive the stated in-game benefit. An interstitial, if available, is restricted to the natural transition after a player has already chosen to end a run; it does not prevent the player from returning to the game map.

### D. Remote studio catalog and external links

The App can retrieve a public, GitHub-hosted catalog to display optional information about our studio's current or upcoming apps. The catalog is an optional enhancement and is cached for offline resilience. Requests to retrieve this content may be processed by GitHub and the network providers involved in delivering it. The App does not send your saved game profile to the catalog.

If you choose to open a Play Store, App Store, website, review, or other external link, the relevant third party's privacy policy applies to activity after you leave the App. The App opens such links only after your direct action.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

- save and restore local game progress and settings;
- provide optional feedback and support;
- diagnose bugs, performance issues, and security concerns;
- show and measure advertising only where enabled and permitted by your privacy choices;
- prevent fraud and abuse;
- provide the optional studio catalog; and
- meet legal, regulatory, and enforcement obligations.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising partners may process identifiers and interaction information to deliver, measure, and protect advertising in accordance with the choices you make and their own terms and policies.

## 4. When information is shared

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the App. These include:

| Recipient or category | Information involved | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform | Advertising identifiers, IP address, ad/app interaction information, diagnostics, and consent choices as applicable | Consent management, advertising delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention |
| Support-record provider, including a Google-hosted support workflow when configured | Feedback or support message, optional email, App version, platform, and screen context | Receiving, organizing, and responding to feedback and support requests |
| GitHub and content-delivery network providers | Standard request data needed to retrieve the optional public studio catalog | Serving optional catalog text and artwork |
| Authorities or professional advisers | Information reasonably necessary in the circumstances | Legal compliance, protection of rights, fraud prevention, or response to lawful requests |

Service providers may process information in countries other than the country in which you live. We take reasonable steps to use providers with appropriate safeguards, but international processing can be subject to the laws of the country where the provider operates.

## 5. Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you clear it, reset it, uninstall the App, or your device/operating-system backup rules remove it.

We retain submitted feedback and support information for up to **24 months** after receipt, unless a longer period is reasonably necessary to resolve an issue, maintain security, comply with law, or establish or defend legal claims. We then delete or anonymize it where reasonably practicable.

Advertising and consent data are retained by the relevant advertising and consent providers under their own policies. You should review those providers' policies for their specific retention practices.

## 6. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or objection to certain processing of your personal information. You may also have a right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent and to complain to a relevant data-protection authority.

To make a privacy request, contact **niks28.consult@gmail.com** with the subject line **"Sudoku Cosmos Privacy Request."** Please include enough information for us to understand and verify the request. We may request reasonable information to protect against unauthorized disclosure or deletion.

You can also use the following controls:

- Do not submit optional feedback or an email address if you do not want us to receive it.
- Clear local App storage or uninstall the App to remove locally stored progress from the device.
- Use the in-App privacy-options control, when available, to review eligible advertising-consent choices.
- Use Android settings to reset or delete the Android advertising ID.

## 7. Children

Sudoku Cosmos is not designed for, marketed to, or knowingly directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at **niks28.consult@gmail.com** so that we can review and take appropriate action.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its data practices, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date when we do. Material changes will be communicated through an updated policy, in-App notice, or another appropriate method before or when the change takes effect, where required.
